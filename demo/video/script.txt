A lot of my learning happens digitally, on PDFs, on papers like this one. So, inspired by Excalidraw, I made Marginalia. Highlight and underline the text. Draw boxes, arrows, anything freehand. Write notes in the margin. And the part I could not find anywhere else: take a figure from page three, and paste it straight onto page one. Resize it, turn it, put it where you want. Close the file, open it tomorrow, and every mark is still yours to move. In light, or in dark.
